About the exhibition

A stage for every inventor, whatever stage you are at

Whether you are a student with a fresh idea, an entrepreneur developing new solutions, or an experienced innovator refining advanced technology, this is your stage to shine.

Hosted by the World Invention Intellectual Property Associations (WIIPA), this two-day international event runs from August 14 to August 15, 2026, featuring exhibitions, competitions and an inspiring award ceremony.

This gathering is not only about showcasing inventions — it is about celebrating the courage to imagine, the determination to create, and the vision to change the world. Every participant, regardless of outcome, gains valuable experience, broader perspectives, and a global community of like-minded innovators.

Evaluation criteria

How the jury scores your invention

Four criteria decide the medals. Beyond them, special jury awards honour outstanding contributions, exceptional creativity and remarkable presentations.

01

Novelty

How original and unique your invention is compared with existing solutions.

02

Inventive Step

The creativity behind your problem-solving, and the improvements it brings to current technologies.

03

Industrial Applicability

The potential for real-world use, production and market impact.

04

Demonstration

The clarity, effectiveness and passion with which you present your invention.

Why take part

Be inspired by experts in design, technology and more

One of the exhibition's greatest strengths is its openness. With no age limit and no restriction on background, young learners and seasoned professionals present their creations side by side.

  • Exchange ideas with inventors, researchers, companies and industry leaders.
  • Learn from expert speakers in design, technology, video production, entrepreneurship and more.
  • Build global networks that foster collaboration and future opportunities.
  • Earn recognition through competition and awards celebrating creativity and innovation.
